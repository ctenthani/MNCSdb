# MNCS Registry V0.9 — sports operations

This release adds reviewed athlete, team, competition, fixture and result records
to the existing association submissions, account management and awards dossiers.
It is a complete package; earlier ZIPs are not needed.

## What associations can do

- Register athletes with private birth dates, discipline, competition category,
  club, district and an explicit public-profile permission decision.
- Register teams; create competitions with dates, category, level and venue.
- Schedule team fixtures in Malawi time; update their round, venue and status.
- Record athlete or team results, placing/outcome, performance, explicitly awarded medal and evidence reference.
  A first-place finish does not automatically claim a gold medal.
- Save, edit and submit records. MNCS can return them with correction instructions.
- Prepare award nominations from ranked athletes. The nominee identity and
  achievements are filled from approved master records and approved results
  dated within the selected calendar awards year. Associations must review the
  text, provide their justification and attach the official supporting PDF.

## What MNCS can do

- Review all associations' sports records; approve, return or archive with a reason.
- Prepare sports drafts for an association as an administrator. Reviewers review
  records but cannot create drafts or change scoring rules.
- Create immutable placing-points rules by association, discipline, category and
  season, with a cap on counting competitions. Each rule has its own leaderboard.
- View server-calculated athlete rankings and export them as CSV.
- Open or close each awards year's nomination submission window. The database
  rejects a new nomination submission when that year is closed.
- See nomination screening counts and open the existing review queue.

The public directory receives approved athlete profiles with permission,
competition records, anonymised results where permission is absent, and an
approved fixture calendar. Birth dates, private evidence and review history
remain accessible only to MNCS and the athlete's association.

## Ranking rules

For each matching scoring rule:

1. Use the **latest approved snapshot** of each placed athlete result. Draft,
   returned and submitted corrections cannot alter published performance.
2. Award the policy's points for the placing. Placings beyond its configured list
   receive zero. Won/Lost/Draw/DNS/DNF/Disqualified results do not earn placing points.
3. Count each athlete's best placing score once per competition. Multiple
   fixture results in that competition cannot multiply ranking points.
4. Add the best N competition scores, where N is the rule's counting cap.
5. Equal totals share a competition rank (1, 1, 3). Name sorting is for display
   and does not decide a tie.

Example: points 10, 8, 6 and best two competitions. An athlete's scores 10, 8, 6
produce 18 points, with 2 of 3 competitions counted. A second athlete with 10, 8
also has 18 points and shares the rank.

Rules are created by MNCS and cannot be edited through the app. A new rule means
a new leaderboard; it does not rewrite existing approved competitions. Match
discipline/category spelling exactly. Different sports are not ranked against
one another. There are no silently assumed international-event multipliers or
automatic medal-based awards decisions. MNCS should agree the sport's points and
counting cap before approving competitions under that rule.

## Upgrade from the working V0.8

1. Export a backup of your existing Supabase data.
2. Run **supabase/INSTALL_ALL.sql** in Supabase SQL Editor. This is the single
   consolidated installer, including V0.9. It preserves existing records,
   profiles and initial-administrator state; it creates no accounts/passwords.
   Do not run every older migration separately.
3. Replace the Git repository's project files with this ZIP's contents. Keep
   index.html and netlify.toml at the root, and netlify/functions intact.
4. Deploy through the Git-connected Netlify project. Keep your working Functions
   variables: SUPABASE_URL, SUPABASE_SECRET_KEY and SITE_ORIGIN. If the public URL
   was marked secret, retain the targeted SECRETS_SCAN_OMIT_KEYS=SUPABASE_URL
   setting described in NETLIFY_DEPLOYMENT_FIX.md. Never exclude the private key.
5. Sign in to Workspace. **Sports operations** and **Rankings** are new tabs.
6. As MNCS administrator, create a scoring rule in Rankings if that sport needs
   athlete ranking. Association representatives can see their association's rules.
7. Create and approve athlete/team records, then a matching competition, then
   fixtures and results. References must be approved before they can be used.
8. In Awards, choose the year, set the junior reference date and explicitly open
   nominations. New/existing cycles default to Closed when this column is added.
9. Test one association account and an MNCS reviewer on the live deployment.
   Confirm isolation, approval, public publishing, rankings and PDF access.

Do not repeat first-administrator setup. Your existing login remains in place.
For a site already configured by an owner, routine operation is entirely on-site.

## Corrections and audit

Editing an approved record starts a correction draft. Its approved snapshot
continues to supply the public directory and rankings until MNCS approves the
replacement. Edits and transitions append actor/time/history records.
Approved discipline/category identities, competition dates/scoring policy and
fixture participants stay fixed. Create a new record for a different identity.
Archives require MNCS and a reason; dependent records must be archived first.
Archives remove the record from public projections and ranking calculations.
Archive history remains private in the master table. There is no restore button
in this release; restoration needs an owner-reviewed database operation.

## Scope and limits

- Fixtures are entered manually, with an exact-start-time team-conflict check.
  Automatic round-robin/bracket generation, duration-overlap checks, match
  official assignments, detailed match statistics and league tables are future work.
- Athletes are registered per discipline/category; this release has no unified
  cross-sport person identity, team roster eligibility engine or transfer workflow.
- Team results are stored and reviewed; athlete placing rankings are the
  implemented ranking system. Sport-specific time/mark rankings need further rules.
- Results use a text performance/score and evidence reference. Upload the relevant
  signed result report through the existing private document-submission workflow.
  This release does not attach a separate PDF directly to each sports result.
- Awards support nomination windows and eligibility screening. Junior nominees
  remain strictly under 20 on the cycle reference date. Approved dossiers are
  not category wins. Operational judge ballots, shortlist approvals, fan polls,
  audited official results and appeals are not implemented. Public vote data
  cannot influence these athlete performance rankings.
- Historical sample records are not migrated into private sports masters.
  Live approved V0.9 records use stable UUIDs; import/duplicate reconciliation
  of any older athlete records needs an explicit owner-reviewed process.
- The isolated demo mirrors common workflows; it does not certify Supabase RLS.
- Password-recovery emails, bulk data imports, notifications and automated backups
  remain outside this release. Existing account creation/reset behavior is retained.
